Questions

What is cloud hosting?

Cloud hosting means your website or app runs on virtual servers in a data centre, instead of one physical machine you own. You can add power or storage in minutes and only pay for what you use.

What's the difference between VPS and shared hosting?

On shared hosting, many websites share one server's resources. A VPS gives you your own guaranteed CPU, memory and storage, so it's faster and more reliable.

How does hourly billing work?

You pay only for the hours a server is running, up to the monthly price. Delete a server after 10 hours and you pay for 10 hours.

Can you move my website from another host?

Yes. Our team moves your site for free on any plan of $24 a month or more, with no downtime.
