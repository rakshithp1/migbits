Questions

How much does it cost to hire a freelancer from Tandem?

Our rates are $70 to $95 an hour depending on the person. For most projects we give a fixed price instead, so you know the total before we start. Projects start at $1,500.

Can I hire just one person?

Yes. Many clients start with one of us for a small job and add others later as the project grows.

Are you a company or independent freelancers?

We're independent freelancers who share clients, tools and standards. You sign one agreement and get one invoice from Tandem.

Where are you based?

Our team lives in London, Lisbon, Berlin and Toronto, and we work with clients in any time zone between them.

Send us a brief.

A few lines is enough. We'll reply within two working days with the right people for the job and a clear price.
