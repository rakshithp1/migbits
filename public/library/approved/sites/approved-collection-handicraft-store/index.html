Questions

Is every piece really handmade?

Yes. Every item is made by hand by the maker named on the page. No mass production and no factories.

Why does my piece look a little different from the photo?

Because it's handmade, each piece has small differences in glaze, colour and texture. That's what makes it one of a kind.

Can I become a maker?

We welcome applications from independent makers. Send us photos of your work and we'll arrange a studio visit.
