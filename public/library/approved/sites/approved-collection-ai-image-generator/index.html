Questions

Can I use AI-generated images for my business?

Yes. On any paid plan you can use your images for ads, websites, products and client work. Free plan images are for personal use.

How does an AI image generator work?

You type a description, and the AI model creates a new image that matches it, based on patterns it learned from millions of licensed images. Each result is new, not copied from one picture.

What kind of images can I make?

Photos, illustrations, 3D renders, patterns, posters and backgrounds. We block harmful content and images of real people without consent.

Are my images private?

On paid plans, your images are private by default. Free plan images may appear in the public gallery.
