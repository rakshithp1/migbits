FAQ

Common questions

What does managed IT services include?

Unlimited help desk support, 24/7 monitoring, security updates, backups, device management and a named account manager, all for one fixed monthly fee per user.

How fast do you respond to support requests?

Critical issues get a response within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day. Standard requests are answered within one business hour. These times are written into your contract.

Can you work with our existing IT team?

Yes. Many clients use our co-managed plan, where we handle monitoring, security and after-hours support while their internal team focuses on projects.

How long does onboarding take?

Most businesses are fully onboarded in two to four weeks, with no downtime during the switch.

Do you support remote and hybrid teams?

Absolutely. We support staff wherever they work, ship pre-configured laptops to new starters and secure home networks.
