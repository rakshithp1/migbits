Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Email hello@brightline.agency and a strategist will reply within one business day.

How long does it take to see results?

Paid ads can bring qualified traffic within the first two weeks. SEO usually shows clear movement in three to six months, depending on your competition and the state of your website.

Do you lock clients into long contracts?

No. After a 90-day starting period, every plan runs month to month with 30 days' notice. We'd rather earn your business every month.

What is the minimum ad budget?

We recommend at least $1,500 a month in ad spend so the platforms have enough data to optimise. We'll tell you honestly if a smaller budget won't work for your goals.

How do you report on performance?

You get a live dashboard, a written monthly report and a review call. We focus on revenue, cost per lead and what we'll do next, not vanity metrics.

Which industries do you work with?

E-commerce, SaaS, professional services, healthcare, hospitality and local businesses. We don't take on two direct competitors in the same city.
