Questions

What is the best way to build a new habit?

Start very small, do it at the same time each day and track it. Tracking works because seeing your streak makes you less likely to break it. Habitly is designed around these three steps.

Is Habitly free?

Yes. You can track up to five habits for free, forever. Premium adds unlimited habits, charts, challenges and backup.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Habitly works on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Android phones and Wear OS watches.

Can I cancel Premium any time?

Yes. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play settings. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
