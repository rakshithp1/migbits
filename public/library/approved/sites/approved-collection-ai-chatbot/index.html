Frequently asked questions

What is an AI chatbot for customer service?

An AI chatbot is a chat window on your website that reads your content and answers customer questions automatically, any time of day. Good chatbots pass difficult questions to someone on your team.

Will the chatbot make up wrong answers?

Mira only answers using content you've added. If it can't find the answer, it tells the customer and hands the chat to your team instead of guessing.

How long does it take to set up?

Most businesses have Mira live in 10 to 30 minutes. You add your website address, test a few questions and paste one line of code.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can install Mira with a plugin for WordPress, Shopify, Wix or Webflow, or by pasting one line of code.

What happens if I go over my conversation limit?

Mira keeps working. We'll email you and suggest the right plan, and you'll never be charged more without agreeing first.
