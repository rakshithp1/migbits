Questions

How do I install a browser extension?

Click "Add to Chrome" on this page, then click "Add extension" in the window that opens. The Tabnook icon appears next to your address bar. It takes about ten seconds.

Does Tabnook slow down my browser?

No, it does the opposite. Saving and closing tabs you don't need frees up memory, so your browser and computer run faster.

Can Tabnook see my browsing history?

No. Tabnook only reads the tabs you choose to save, and stores them on your device unless you turn on sync.

Which browsers does it work with?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Firefox and Safari on Mac and Windows.
